I am a Research Scientist at ByteDance. My research concerns AI for infrastructure, including AIOps, anomaly detection and log analysis, which aims to provide more reliable cloud systems.

I received my Ph.D. degree from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, under the supervision of [Prof. Michael R. Lyu](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/lyu/). I got my Bachelor's (2018) and Master's (2020) degrees from Sun Yat-sen University in [InPlus Lab](http://inpluslab.com/), under the supervision of [Prof. Zibin Zheng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hmBe63gAAAAJ).

Our project [LogPAI](https://github.com/logpai) provides an end-to-end solution to intelligently manage and analyze logs for modern production systems using machine learning techniques. Feel free to contact us if you have any questions.
